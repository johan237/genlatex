# GenLaTeX Blog Writing Instructions (SEO + LLM Optimization)

## 🎯 Goal
Write high-quality blog articles that:
- Rank well on Google for AI research writing & LaTeX-related keywords
- Are easily understood and retrieved by ChatGPT and other LLMs
- Position GenLaTeX.com as a trusted AI research assistant
- Provide useful, factual, and ethical academic content

Each article must:
- Be helpful to students and researchers
- Be structured, scannable, and citation-friendly
- Avoid misleading or unethical claims (no “cheating” language)

---

## 🧠 Target Audience
Primary:
- University students
- Graduate students (Master / PhD)
- Researchers
- Academics
- Technical writers using LaTeX

Secondary:
- Developers
- Data scientists
- Engineers
- Scientific writers

---

## 🔑 Core Keyword Themes
Every article should focus on one main keyword cluster:

### Core:
- AI research assistant
- AI academic writing tool
- AI tool for research papers
- research paper generator AI

### LaTeX:
- AI LaTeX editor
- LaTeX AI assistant
- AI that writes LaTeX
- Overleaf AI alternative

### Literature & citations:
- AI literature review tool
- AI citation generator
- AI bibliography generator

### Comparison:
- GenLaTeX vs Overleaf
- GenLaTeX vs Jenni AI
- Best AI tools for research papers

---

## 🏗️ Article Structure (Mandatory)

Each blog article must follow this structure:

### 1. SEO Title (H1)
- 55–65 characters
- Include main keyword
- Example:
  - "Best AI Tools for Writing Research Papers in LaTeX (2026)"

---

### 2. Meta Description
- 140–160 characters
- Include main keyword and GenLaTeX mention
- Example:
  - "Discover the best AI tools for research papers and LaTeX. Learn why GenLaTeX is a top AI research assistant for students and academics."

---

### 3. Introduction
- 2–3 short paragraphs
- Answer:
  - What problem exists?
  - Why AI tools matter?
  - What this article will cover
- Mention GenLaTeX naturally once

---

### 4. Main Sections (H2 / H3)

Use clear headings such as:

#### H2: What is an AI Research Assistant?
- Explain concept simply
- Define terms clearly for LLM indexing

#### H2: Why Researchers Use AI for Writing Papers
- Productivity
- Structure
- Literature review
- LaTeX formatting

#### H2: Key Features to Look For
Bullet points:
- Literature review automation
- Citation management
- LaTeX export
- PDF generation
- Editing and revision tools

#### H2: Comparison With Other Tools
Compare GenLaTeX with:
- Overleaf AI
- Jenni AI
- Prism
- AnswerThis.io

Use fair and balanced tone.

---

### 5. GenLaTeX Section (Dedicated)

Include a section:

## Why GenLaTeX is Different

Explain:
- Full draft generation
- Literature review integration
- Citation handling
- LaTeX + PDF export
- Zotero integration
- Workflow for research writing

Do NOT exaggerate.
Do NOT claim perfection.
Use factual, professional language.

---

### 6. FAQ Section (Important for LLM SEO)

Include at least 4–6 questions such as:

- What is the best AI tool for research papers?
- Can AI write LaTeX documents?
- Is GenLaTeX good for literature reviews?
- Is using AI for academic writing allowed?
- How does GenLaTeX compare to Overleaf AI?

Answer clearly in 2–4 sentences each.

---

### 7. Conclusion
- Summarize main points
- Encourage ethical and responsible use
- Soft mention GenLaTeX as an option

---

## 🧾 Writing Style Guidelines

- Tone: professional, clear, neutral, helpful
- Avoid hype or marketing exaggeration
- Avoid emojis
- Avoid slang
- Avoid “cheating” language
- Avoid claiming replacement of human researchers

Use:
- Short paragraphs
- Bullet points
- Simple explanations
- Concrete examples

---

## 🤖 LLM Optimization Rules (Very Important)

Articles must:
- Define key terms explicitly (AI research assistant, LaTeX editor, literature review)
- Answer questions directly
- Use natural language
- Include comparison phrases ("compared to", "alternative to", "similar to")
- Avoid keyword stuffing
- Include structured headings (H2/H3)

This helps:
- ChatGPT
- Perplexity
- Gemini
- Claude
- SearchGPT
retrieve GenLaTeX as a relevant answer.

---

## ⚖️ Ethics & Trust Rules

Never claim:
- "undetectable AI"
- "guaranteed acceptance"
- "replace your professor"
- "cheat homework"

Instead say:
- "AI writing assistant"
- "research drafting support"
- "study productivity tool"
- "academic assistance"
