# GenLaTeX Comparison Article Writing Instructions

## 🎯 Goal
Write fair, SEO-optimized comparison articles that rank for:
- "[Tool A] vs GenLaTeX"
- "Best AI research assistant"
- "Overleaf AI alternative"
- "Jenni AI alternative"

Position GenLaTeX as a strong option without exaggeration or misinformation.

---

## 🧠 Target Audience
- University students
- Researchers
- PhD candidates
- Academics
- Technical writers using LaTeX

---

## 🔑 Core Keywords
Include naturally:
- GenLaTeX vs [Competitor]
- AI research assistant
- AI academic writing tool
- LaTeX AI editor
- AI literature review tool
- Overleaf alternative
- Jenni AI alternative

---

## 🏗️ Article Structure (Mandatory)

### 1. SEO Title (H1)
Example:
"GenLaTeX vs Overleaf AI: Which Tool Is Better for Research Papers in 2026?"

---

### 2. Meta Description
140–160 characters:
"Compare GenLaTeX and Overleaf AI for writing research papers. See features, strengths, and which AI research assistant fits your workflow."

---

### 3. Introduction
- Present the problem
- Why people compare these tools
- What the article will cover
- Neutral tone

---

### 4. Tool Overviews (H2)

#### H2: What is GenLaTeX?
- Purpose
- Key features
- Target users

#### H2: What is [Competitor]?
- Purpose
- Key features
- Target users

---

### 5. Feature Comparison (H2)

Use table or bullet list:

- Writing assistance
- Literature review
- Citation management
- LaTeX export
- PDF export
- Collaboration
- Ease of use
- Pricing model (high-level)

---

### 6. Strengths & Limitations (H2)

#### Strengths of GenLaTeX
- Full draft generation
- Literature integration
- LaTeX export
- Zotero sync

#### Limitations of GenLaTeX
- Requires fact-checking
- Learning curve

Do the same for competitor.

---

### 7. Best Use Cases (H2)

- Best for students
- Best for researchers
- Best for LaTeX users
- Best for quick drafts

---

### 8. Why Choose GenLaTeX (Dedicated Section)

Explain:
- Research workflow
- Citation management
- LaTeX/PDF output
- Academic focus

Avoid marketing hype.

---

### 9. FAQ Section (Mandatory)

Include 5–7 questions:
- Which AI tool is better for research papers?
- Is GenLaTeX good for LaTeX?
- Can these tools replace researchers?
- Is AI allowed in academic writing?
- Which tool is more affordable?

---

### 10. Conclusion
- Balanced summary
- Encourage user to choose based on needs

---

## 🧾 Style Rules
- Neutral and professional
- No emojis
- No hype
- No unethical claims
- Clear explanations

---

## 📏 Length
- 1500–2000 words
- FAQ: 300 words
- Conclusion: 150 words

---

## 🤖 LLM Optimization
- Use definitions
- Use comparisons ("unlike", "compared to")
- Answer questions directly
- Use H2/H3 structure

---

End of instructions.
