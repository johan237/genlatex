# Third-party components

GenLaTeX's original source is MIT licensed. Dependencies and imported assets
retain their respective licences; the root licence does not relicense them.

The optional SwiftLaTeX browser engine is distributed by its upstream project
under AGPL-3.0. It is not bundled in this checkout. The installer preserves its
upstream licence and records its source, release, and checksums alongside the
downloaded files. Keep those files and upstream source references when deploying
the optional engine. See https://github.com/SwiftLaTeX/SwiftLaTeX.

PDF.js, React, Next.js, and the other npm packages keep their licence notices
in their distributions. TeX packages downloaded by the engine have separate
licences from their publishers.
